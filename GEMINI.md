# ZW Macro Valuation Guidelines
When calculating or referencing the Macro Accumulation Zone or Fair Value for Wheat (ZW) on the CBOT dashboard, always use the following parameters:
- **Base Price (Giá Gốc)**: 550¢ (Assumes normal conditions: Oil $70, DXY 100, no war).
- **Valuation Formula**: FV = Base Price (550) + Oil Premium + DXY Premium + War Premium
  - *Oil Premium*: +2 cents for every $1 above $70/barrel.
  - *DXY Premium*: -5 cents for every 1 point above 100.
  - *War Premium (Rủi ro địa chính trị)*: F8 Score * 5 cents.
- Do NOT use 520¢ or any other base. 550¢ is the hard baseline for ZW.
